A complete product, not just a VPN server

The goal was to close the gap between marketing, payment and technical access delivery while keeping one understandable journey for the user.

01 · BRAND

Identity and website

A standalone KREM VPN brand, visual system, responsive site, help and clear terms — kept separate from the Kremlinet brand.

02 · TELEGRAM

The bot as an account

Navigation, trial, purchase, current expiry, existing link, device setup and a clear route to support.

03 · PAYMENTS

YooKassa and a dedicated ledger

The order is recorded before provider hand-off. Verification checks amount, currency, brand and order; replay cannot extend access twice.

04 · DELIVERY

Happ and Incy

One HTTPS address serves both primary clients and a neutral import format with expiry headers and no-store protection.

05 · INFRASTRUCTURE

Several connection paths

Multiple protocols and geographic exits provide fallbacks. Monitoring tests actual HTTPS transfer, not only process health.

06 · OPERATIONS

Monitoring and recovery

Checks separate subscription delivery from traffic, preserve working processes and avoid automatic “repairs” after one ambiguous signal.

The complexity was not one integration

Payment, delivery and VPN traffic can fail independently, so the control layer cannot be a single green light.

A replay must not add time twice

Events and requests keep stable identities. Pending, replay and late success are handled without a second fulfilment.

A healthy process is not a healthy route

Monitoring separately tests delivery, TLS, services and HTTPS through each profile. The latest run kept three failed DE paths visible.

Two brands cannot share one ledger identity

KREM and Kremlinet share infrastructure but retain separate bots, customer URLs, orders and access rights.
